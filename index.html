CAMPUS PEDICAB

Neust's Pedicab

Essential Passenger Information

Pedicab Fare

P10

Flat rate per ride to any campus building or facility.

Operating Hours

Regular Schedule:
7:00 AM – 4:30 PM

*Some drivers may accept passengers until 5:00 PM.

Where to Find Pedicabs

7:00 AM – 9:00 AM:

Pedicabs are lined up near the Campus Guardhouse (Gate #58) inside the university entrance.

After 9:00 AM:

Pedicabs usually wait in front of the campus outside the main university entrance.

Why It Is Convenient to Use Pedicabs Inside the Campus

Wide Campus Area: The Sumacab Campus is large and spread out. Pedicabs make travel faster between buildings and facilities.
Safe and Eco-Friendly: Pedicabs produce no harmful emissions and reduce fast-moving vehicles inside the campus.
Convenient During Hot or Rainy Weather: Provides protection from weather elements and helps carry heavy bags or projects.
Affordable Transportation: Budget-friendly flat rates for short distances within the campus.
Support for Local Drivers: Provides local drivers with an income source while assisting students and staff.
